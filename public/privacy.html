Nico Puddle Painter Privacy Policy

Nico Puddle Painter is made for children aged 3 to 6. It collects no personal information, has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

Progress and the buildings in "My Town" are saved only on the device. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (StoreKit); we never see payment details. Purchases and links are only reachable by grown-ups behind a parental gate.

Contact: see the support page.
